# DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

**Status: draft, not in force.** This text is **not** the license of CodeGraph-Sci. The license in force for the
community path is the unmodified PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 in `LICENSE`. This draft records a stricter rule
that the copyright holder may want, so that qualified software/IP counsel can review it. It must not be presented as
legally final, used for any release, or merged into `LICENSE` before that review.

## Intended rule

> Any use by a for-profit business in its professional activities requires a commercial license.

PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 already limits use to noncommercial purposes, personal uses and noncommercial
organizations. The intended rule makes the business case explicit, whatever the purpose of a particular use (for
example internal exploration of a company's own code, which may be argued to be "noncommercial" in isolation).

## Possible approach (for counsel)

1. Keep PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 unchanged and state the policy in `LICENSING.md` and in the commercial agreement
   only (no custom license); or
2. Use a separate, custom community license drafted by counsel, built around entity-based conditions; or
3. Add a short, separately titled additional-permission or clarification document, if counsel confirms it is
   compatible with PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (the standard text itself must never be edited).

## Questions to settle

- Definitions of "business", "commercial use", "for-profit entity" and "affiliate" (PolyForm's "your company"
  definition may be reused).
- Freelancers and sole proprietors; consultants using the software for clients.
- Non-profits; universities and research laboratories funded by companies (PolyForm's "Noncommercial Organizations"
  clause permits them "regardless of the source of funding").
- Internal enterprise use, evaluation by companies (free evaluation agreement?).
- Redistribution and modifications; patents; warranty disclaimer and limitation of liability.
- External contributions: CLA or DCO.
- Third-party dependency compatibility and local AI model licenses.
- Governing law and jurisdiction; commercial support and maintenance terms.
