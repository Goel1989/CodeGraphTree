## What a commercial license provides

- The legal right to use, and as agreed to integrate or redistribute, **the same software** with **the same
  features** as the community path. Nothing is unlocked technically: there is no license key and no activation.
- Optional services that may be agreed separately: support, maintenance, integration, domain packs for in-house
  codes, training, code audits.

## What it does not cover

- Third-party components keep their own licenses (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).
- An optional local AI model keeps its own license (Apache 2.0 or MIT only), which continues to apply to the user.
- The code bases you analyse keep their own licenses.
