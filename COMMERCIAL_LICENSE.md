## What a commercial license provides

- The legal right to integrate or redistribute **the same software** with **the same features** without the
  obligations of the GPL, as agreed. Nothing is unlocked technically: there is no license key and no activation.
- Optional services that may be agreed separately: support, maintenance, warranty, integration, domain packs for
  in-house codes, training, code audits.

## What it does not cover

- Third-party components keep their own licenses (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`); in particular Qt and PySide6 remain under
  their LGPL-3.0 terms unless the licensee obtains a commercial Qt license.
- An optional local AI model keeps its own license (Apache 2.0 or MIT only), which continues to apply to the user.
- The code bases you analyse keep their own licenses.
